# Learned Reranker: LightGBM LambdaRank vs. the Hand-Weighted Blend

Generated by `scripts/train_learned_reranker.py` on 2026-10-19. Cohort: the 1,000-user reporting cohort (seed 42) the ablation uses, NDCG@10 with each user's training history excluded. Intervals are 95% percentile bootstrap (users resampled, 1,000 resamples); differences are paired (same users under both rankings), so an interval that spans zero is a tie.

## Setup

- **Candidates.** Each user's top 200 items under the served weighted blend (mf 0.048 / knn 0.952 / pop 0.0003, the Task 0.6 re-tune). The hand-weighted row is the blend's own order over those candidates, so it is the ablation's `hybrid_weighted_tuned` row; the learned rows reorder the same candidates. The held-out item is among the candidates for 37.0% of cohort users, which caps what any reranker can reach.
- **Training users.** 4,000 validation users outside the reporting cohort (zero overlap), of whom 1,545 have their held-out item among the candidates; only those carry a LambdaRank gradient. Each seed splits them 80 / 20 into training and early-stopping users (early stopping on NDCG@10). Best iterations, all signals: 148, 48, 49; CF only: 102, 14, 37.
- **Label.** 1 for the user's held-out interaction, 0 for every other candidate. Every feature is computed from the train split: MF and kNN are the train-split artifacts, popularity is counted on train rows, and the content and metadata affinities are profiles of the user's train history (`build_feature_frame` refuses a user whose held-out item is in that history).
- **Features.** CF: blend score and rank, kNN score and rank, MF score and rank, popularity score and percentile. Content and metadata (the signals Stage 2 uses, as user-profile affinities): synopsis TF-IDF and synopsis neural cosine to the user's history, genre and theme overlap with it, MAL score, log members, episodes, year, type, history length.
- **Seeds.** 42, 43, 44: each changes the train / early-stopping user split and LightGBM's bagging seed. The reporting cohort does not change.

## Decision rule

The plan's rule: wire the ranker into the app only if its NDCG@10 gain over the hand-weighted blend exceeds the seed-to-seed spread **and** the paired bootstrap interval on the difference excludes 0.

| ranker | mean gain over hand-weighted | seed-to-seed spread (max - min) | paired CI excludes 0 (all seeds) | verdict |
|:---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| all signals | +0.0273 | 0.0059 | yes | **ship behind `RERANKER_MODE=learned`** |
| CF signals only | +0.0025 | 0.0034 | no | (diagnostic only) |

## Conclusion

Learning a ranker over the CF signals alone gives 0.0683 (+0.0025 vs. 0.0658), a tie: the Optuna-tuned weights already extract what the three CF scores can give. With the content and metadata signals added it reaches 0.0931 (+0.0273 over the hand-weighted blend's 0.0658), larger than the seed-to-seed spread (0.0059) with every seed's paired interval above zero, so it meets the rule and is wired behind `RERANKER_MODE=learned`. The most-used features are `synopsis_tfidf_sim`, `blend_rank`, `knn_rank`. RRF over the same three sources scores 0.0547. Any reranker of these candidates is capped by their recall: the held-out item is in the top 200 for 37.0% of users, so a better first stage (the implicit ALS in `reports/mf_diagnostic.md` ranks far better on its own) moves the ceiling more than reordering can.
